A chunk is the span every analyzer describes and every search hit returns. Cut in the wrong
places and a scene description straddles two unrelated moments, a sentence is split mid-clause,
and retrieval returns spans that are technically correct and practically useless.

## The four policies

VIDRA decides chunk boundaries with one of four policies.

| Policy | Cuts on | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `scene` | Visual scene changes — a big jump in the frames' colour histogram (HSV), checked every 5th frame | The picture |
| `vad` | Pauses in speech, from the Whisper transcript | The soundtrack |
| `speaker` | Changes of speaker, from diarization | The soundtrack |
| `uniform` | Nothing — fixed 20-second spans | Nothing |

For every policy except `uniform`, spans shorter than **5 s** are merged into a neighbour, and
spans longer than **20 s** are split evenly. A final chunk under a quarter of the nominal length is
folded into the one before it.

In practice: the 5-minute tutorial used as a reference here came out as 24 `scene` chunks between
5.5 and 19.7 seconds, with a median of 13.8.

## What the upload dialog picks

The upload dialog keeps the original three modes. The backend maps each one onto a policy:

| Upload choice | Policy |
|---|---|
| Preset `Audio + video` (default) | `scene` |
| Preset `Video-led` | `scene` |
| Preset `Audio-led` | `vad` |
| Custom weights | The heaviest-weighted signal: `speaker` → `speaker`, `silence` → `vad`, `cut` or `semantic` → `scene` |
| Fixed interval | `uniform` |

If the chosen policy needs a signal that wasn't selected — `vad` without a speech analyzer, or
`scene` with no picture analyzer — the backend falls back to the other one, then to `uniform`.
A video with no audio track falls back to `uniform` automatically.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Known limitation:** the chunk bounds are fixed at 5–20 s and fixed intervals are always 20 s.
  The dialog's *shortest*, *longest* and *seconds per chunk* fields are accepted and stored, but
  VIDRA's `workflow()` does not take them yet, so they have no effect.
</Callout>

## Which one to use

| Footage | Choice |
|---|---|
| Edited video, tutorials, screen recordings | `Audio + video` (scene cuts) |
| Podcasts, calls, lectures with a static camera | `Audio-led` (speech pauses) |
| Meetings and interviews where turns matter | Custom weights with `speaker` highest |
| Unbroken CCTV, or comparing two videos like-for-like | Fixed interval |

## Where it shows up

The policy is stored as the video's `chunk_config` (`scene`, `vad`, `speaker` or `uniform`) and on
the row in `video_core`. The full upload choice is stored as `ingest_config` and replayed verbatim
on re-index.

## Changing it later

Re-indexing with a different chunking re-runs the pipeline into the same video folder. Chunk ids
are positions within one chunking — chunk 12 under `scene` is a different moment from chunk 12
under `vad` — so never keep a `chunk_id` across a re-index.
