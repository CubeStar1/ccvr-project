An analyzer describes one chunk. An aggregator reads **every** chunk at once and produces a
conclusion about the whole video — a summary, a chapter breakdown, who talked most, what
happened in order.

This is the part retrieval structurally cannot do. "How much of this is talking?" cannot be
answered by finding the five most relevant moments; it needs all of them, counted.

## The eleven

Each aggregator has a **tier**, which is what it costs to run.

| Aggregate | Tier | Needs | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stats` | free | – | Duration, chunk count and lengths, frames sampled, words, words per minute |
| `coverage` | free | – | Which chunks have picture, sound, both or neither |
| `speaker_stats` | free | `diarization` | Talk time, share, turns, handovers and the dominant speaker |
| `ner` | local | – | Named entities in speech and descriptions — **GLiNER small v2.1** |
| `sentiment` | local | – | Sentiment per chunk, the mean, and turning points — **DistilBERT SST-2** |
| `summary` | llm | – | A whole-video summary, topics, setting and notable moments |
| `chapters` | llm | – | Consecutive chunks grouped into titled, summarised sections |
| `events` | llm | – | Discrete timestamped events, each with a kind |
| `entities` | llm | `people` | People linked across chunks, each with a written account |
| `object_entities` | llm | `object_detection` | Objects linked across chunks |
| `text_entities` | llm | `ocr` | On-screen text (titles, labels, slides) linked across chunks |

"Free" is plain counting. "Local" runs a small model on your machine. "llm" calls
**OpenAI `gpt-6-luna`**.

<Callout type="info">
  An aggregator whose analyzer the video lacks is **skipped, not failed**. Skipped ids come back
  in the re-run result's `skipped` map with the reason.
</Callout>

## Choosing the tier

`VIDRA_TIER` in `backend/.env` sets the costliest tier an ingest runs:

| `VIDRA_TIER` | Runs |
|---|---|
| `free` | `stats`, `coverage`, `speaker_stats` |
| `local` | plus `ner`, `sentiment` |
| `llm` (default) | plus `summary`, `chapters`, `events` and the three entity aggregates |

The agent's `ask_video` tool leans on `summary` and `chapters`, so keep the default unless cost
matters more than whole-video answers.

## Notable outputs

### `summary`

```json
{
  "summary": "The video is a fast-paced visual introduction to modern HTML, presented through animated title cards and code-editor/browser demonstrations…",
  "topics": ["HTML document structure and boilerplate", "Elements, tags, headings, paragraphs, and nesting"],
  "setting": "An animated instructional presentation using code-editor and browser examples…",
  "notable": ["Code is repeatedly paired with a browser preview to show how markup renders."],
  "chunks_read": 24,
  "word_count": 209
}
```

### `chapters`

Each chapter has a `title`, a `summary`, its `chunk_ids`, and `start` / `end` in seconds.
`covers_all_chunks` says whether every chunk landed in a chapter.

### `events`

```json
{
  "events": [{ "what": "An animated introduction presents HTML… and displays “LEARN HTML IN JUST 5 MINUTES.”",
               "kind": "change", "chunk_id": 0, "start": 0.0, "end": 13.834 }],
  "count": 36
}
```

The backend also copies `what` to `event` and `kind` to `category`, the names the frontend reads.

### `stats`

Free, and the right answer to "how long / how many / how much talking". Contains `duration_s`,
`chunks`, `chunk_s` (`min` / `median` / `max`), the chunking `policy`, `frames_sampled`, `words`,
`chunks_with_speech` and `words_per_minute`.

### Entities

`entities`, `object_entities` and `text_entities` share one method: every sighting of a person,
object or piece of text is embedded, sightings are linked into one entity when they match closely
enough, and then the LLM writes an account of each entity across the video. Each entity carries
`label`, `appearances`, `chunk_ids`, first and last seen, and `account`.

<Callout type="warn">
  Entity ids are meaningful within one video only. Two videos of the same person produce two
  unrelated entities.
</Callout>

## Running them

- Automatically, at the end of every ingest, up to `VIDRA_TIER`.
- On demand, with [`POST /videos/{video_id}/aggregates`](/docs/api/aggregates) — returns `202` and
  a job id.
- From the frontend, on the video detail page, which proxies the same call.
