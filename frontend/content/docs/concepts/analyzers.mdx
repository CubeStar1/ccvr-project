An analyzer is one pass over every chunk. Which analyzers ran on a video decides what that video
can be asked at all: one analysed without speech has nothing to quote; one without `people` has
no linked people.

Analyzers are selected **per upload**, because their costs differ.

## The six

There are two kinds. The two **speech** analyzers process the soundtrack. The four **picture**
analyzers are each a VIDRA *sampler*: a rule for which frames of a chunk are worth showing to the
vision model.

| id | Kind | How frames or audio are chosen | Billed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `default_video` | picture — `uniform` sampler | Evenly spaced frames across the chunk | A vision call per chunk |
| `ocr` | picture — `text` sampler | Frames where text appears or changes, found by the **PP-OCRv3 DB** text detector (OpenCV) | A vision call per chunk with text |
| `people` | picture — `yolo` sampler | **YOLO11n** finds each person, **CLIP ViT-B/32** embeds each crop, and a frame is kept when the people in it look different from the last kept frame | A vision call per chunk with people |
| `object_detection` | picture — `objects` sampler | Frames where **YOLOv8s-World** detections change | A vision call per chunk with objects |
| `transcript` | speech | **faster-whisper `small`** | Free (local) |
| `diarization` | speech | faster-whisper plus **pyannote `speaker-diarization-3.1`** | Free (local) |

Every picture analyzer drops near-duplicate frames before anything is sent.

<Callout type="info">
  The local detectors only **choose frames**. They never label what's in them — the vision model
  does that, looking at the frames themselves. A detector guess written onto an image tends to
  get repeated back by the model, right or wrong.
</Callout>

## What the vision model returns

For each chunk and each picture analyzer, the chosen frames go to **OpenAI `gpt-6-luna`** with a
fixed JSON schema. The answer is a prose `description` plus structured fields: `setting`,
`people`, `objects`, `actions`, `changes`, `tags` and `visible_text`.

The backend presents that per analyzer, in the shapes the frontend was written against:

| Analyzer | Chunk output |
|---|---|
| `default_video` | `description`, `setting`, `people[]`, `objects[]`, `actions[]`, `tags[]` |
| `ocr` | `texts[]` of `{text, context}` — each piece of visible text and where it appears — plus a `summary` |
| `people` | `people[]`, `people_count`, plus a `summary` |
| `object_detection` | `detections[]` of `{object}`, plain `objects[]`, plus a `summary` |
| `transcript` | `text` |
| `diarization` | `text`, `turns[]` of `{speaker, start, end, text}`, `speakers[]` |

## `transcript` and `diarization`

<Callout type="warn">
  These two are **mutually exclusive**. `diarization` is a superset: the same Whisper text plus
  speaker turns. Selecting both is a `400`; `GET /analyzers` returns `exclusive_groups` so the
  upload dialog can grey the option out.
</Callout>

Whisper runs over the whole track once, with voice-activity filtering to cut down on text
hallucinated from music or room tone. Diarization also runs once over the whole track, so
`SPEAKER_00` is the same person throughout a video. Speaker labels are not consistent across
videos.

## Choosing a set

| Footage | Reasonable selection |
|---|---|
| Interview, meeting, podcast | `default_video` + `diarization` |
| Lecture, tutorial or screen recording | `default_video` + `diarization` + `ocr` |
| CCTV / surveillance | `default_video` + `people` (+ `object_detection`) |
| Silent b-roll | `default_video` |
| Cheapest useful pass | `transcript` alone — free, but nothing visual is searchable |

## Adding an analyzer

On the backend, a picture analyzer is one entry in `ANALYZERS` and one in `SAMPLER_OF` in
`backend/engine.py`, naming a VIDRA sampler. The frontend's upload dialog builds its list from
`GET /api/core/capabilities`, so nothing in the frontend changes.
