`/projects/{projectId}/videos/{videoId}` is the explorer for a single video. Where the chat
answers questions, this page shows what the answers are made of.

It loads from `GET /api/videos/{id}/details`, which returns the row, the backend's own metadata,
every chunk with every analyzer's output attached, and every stored aggregate. Chunks are paged
out of the backend 500 at a time and walked until its `total` is satisfied — the API paginates
because an agent reading chunks wants a few; this page is the opposite case.

<Callout type="info">
  Sources settle independently. A video analysed without `people` has no `entities` aggregate; a
  video mid-ingest has no chunks yet. Neither is a failure, and neither blanks the parts that did
  load — per-source errors are listed and everything else still renders.
</Callout>

## Tabs

### Overview

The video-level picture: player, poster, duration, chunk count, the analyzers that ran, and the
aggregates that exist. This is where `summary`, `chapters`, `events` and `stats` are rendered —
the summary with its topics, chapters as titled spans, events as a timestamped list, stats as
counts.

### Chunks

Every chunk in order, with each analyzer's output for it: the scene description and its facets,
on-screen text, detected objects, and the people seen. This is the raw material of every search
hit, shown unabridged.

### Speech

The transcript, attributed when the video was analysed with `diarization`. Alongside it,
`speaker_stats` (talk time, turns, handovers, share per speaker) and `sentiment` when those
aggregates ran.

### Entities

People linked across the whole video: each one's description, the account of what they did,
first and last seen, and appearance count.

Only present when the video was analysed with `people`. Some panels were built for aggregates the
original backend had (`novelty`, `entity_timelines`, `cooccurrence`); those sections stay empty.

### Raw

The stored output as returned, for when you need to see exactly what was produced rather than a
rendering of it.

## Re-running aggregates

The page can re-run video-level passes without re-analysing anything, through
`POST /api/videos/{id}/aggregates`. Aggregates read stored chunk output rather than the video,
so this is the cheap half of re-processing.

<Callout type="warn">
  Six aggregates bill LLM calls — `summary`, `chapters`, `events`, `entities`, `object_entities`
  and `text_entities`. That is why re-running is a deliberate action rather than something the
  page does on load.
</Callout>

## Deleting and re-indexing

Both are available here as well as from the project grid, and behave identically — see
[Adding videos](/docs/workspace/ingesting-video).
